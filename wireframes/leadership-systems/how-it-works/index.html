If we can't assist, we'll point you to support

If we are unable to facilitate your matter, we will explain why and provide free supportive referrals. Suggested referrals may include:

  • Maryland's Consumer Protection Division
  • Maryland Courts Self‑Help Center
  • People's Law Library
  • Community Mediation Maryland
  • MPME‑rostered ADR practitioners
  • Better Business Bureau
  • Crisis Text Line

Before you begin

  • We provide neutral ADR services only. We do not provide legal advice, legal representation, or legal advocacy.
